Current tools

In the workshop

Experimental prototypes · playtesting

These are working experiments, not finished public tools. We're testing how the writing-crawl format feels before building a reusable system around it. Feedback on pacing, clarity, repetition, choices, and anything confusing is especially helpful.

The Midnight Train

A short hybrid word crawl: explore a train that shouldn't exist, collect Tickets and Clues, and find a way to the destination.

Status: Early playtesting and design notes.

Open the crawl prototype →

The Lost Woods

A longer branching writing adventure with choices, discoveries, flags, conditional encounters, and multiple possible endings.

Status: Prototype built; gathering playtest feedback before refining the crawl engine.

What's brewing

A few things on the way

Prompt Generator / Prompt Deck

A flexible Who / What / When / Where / Why / How system that can generate ingredients through dice, cards, or a mix of both, with future printable decks in mind.

Book Page Generator

Turn a pasted excerpt into a novel-style page image for sharing, with configurable typography, page styles, chapter details, and optional page numbering.

Writing Games

Small, customizable game mechanics for writing challenges, prompt crawls, timed rounds, and community events.

Challenge Tools

Build and track writing challenges with dates, cadences, themes, ingredients, constraints, goals, and submission links.

Stream Widgets

Small browser-source widgets for current activity, writing goals, session progress, breaks, and other stream-friendly displays.

Story Utilities

Randomizers, sensory prompts, writing math, session notes, and other little helpers.

The philosophy

Built to stay useful

✦

Writer-first, platform-neutral, local-first

The goal is a collection of small tools that can work in a browser, on a website, in OBS or Streamlabs, or alongside whatever writing software a writer already uses. They should not need to read the manuscript to be useful.
